# Adoption Turnaround

**Helped increase statewide platform utilization from approximately 12% to 22% in roughly 90 days.**

*Professional case study | Generalized reconstruction*

## The Problem

A public-sector technology program served more than 500 district-level customers with varying readiness, staffing, workflows, and stakeholder engagement. Access and implementation did not consistently translate into routine use.

## My Contribution

I connected implementation support, practical training, and stakeholder coordination to help districts move from platform access to meaningful use.

- **Connected training to daily work:** focused enablement on how the platform supported customer responsibilities and workflows.
- **Surfaced adoption barriers:** used customer conversations and utilization signals to identify where clarification or support was needed.
- **Coordinated action:** translated customer questions and workflow needs into follow-through with internal teams.
- **Supported a distributed portfolio:** used repeatable communication and enablement while accounting for differences in district readiness.
- **Bridged launch and ongoing use:** treated post-launch questions as part of adoption management.

## Result

| Measure | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Program environment | 500+ district-level customers |
| Starting utilization | ~12% |
| Ending utilization | ~22% |
| Change | ~10 percentage points |
| Period | ~90 days |

This was a program-level utilization improvement supported by coordinated customer-success work. Utilization indicates use; it does not, by itself, establish downstream educational or safety outcomes.

## The Judgment Behind the Work

**Usage data identifies where to look. Customer context explains what to do next.**

Low utilization can reflect workflow friction, unclear ownership, training needs, or competing priorities. Understanding those conditions made the response more useful than a uniform engagement campaign.

**Capabilities demonstrated:** adoption strategy, public-sector customer success, enablement, distributed portfolio management, stakeholder alignment, and cross-functional coordination.
