## Customer Outcome Reliability

**Can the organization reliably turn a customer promise into a customer outcome?**

My framework examines the transitions between **Promise, Delivery, Adoption, and Outcome**. Each transition can reveal a gap: expectations that were never aligned, readiness that was assumed, usage that stalls after launch, or activity that does not produce the value the customer needs.

The public tools provide three ways to examine those gaps:

| Tool | Use it to |
| --- | --- |
| [Customer Outcome Reliability Model](./frameworks/customer-outcome-reliability-model.md) | Examine where the customer journey loses continuity |
| [Diagnostic Snapshot](./templates/outcome-reliability-diagnostic-snapshot.md) | Identify where internal status and customer experience may diverge |
| [Failure Signals](./playbooks/outcome-reliability-failure-signals.md) | Recognize recurring patterns in launch gaps, unresolved issues, escalations, and unclear ownership |

## How I Work

- **Investigate the customer experience.** Compare internal status with what customers can actually accomplish.
- **Find the underlying blocker.** Separate symptoms, causes, and dependencies before choosing the intervention.
- **Make execution explicit.** Give meaningful work an owner, next action, decision point, and timing.
- **Verify the outcome.** Confirm that completed work addressed the customer's problem.
- **Keep people accountable.** Use data, automation, and AI to support judgment and follow-through.
